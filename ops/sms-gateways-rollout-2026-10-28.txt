NISTER SMS gateways and admin registration — 2026-10-28 UTC

priority, global delivery pause/resume, persistent health cooldowns and failover.

public signup, admin broadcast and shell quota-alert senders. Broadcasts retain
100-recipient batches; partial or uncertain sends are not automatically duplicated.

user registration. New users start unpaid, with zero wallet balance and a maximum

turn a committed registration into an apparent failure.
