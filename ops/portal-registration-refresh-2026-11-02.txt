Nister portal registration and interface refresh - 2026-11-02

Public registration: https://pay.nister.org/register.php
Canonical signup API: https://api.nister.org/hotspot/signup.php

An explicit global SMS pause now permits NEW-account registration without an
OTP. Resuming SMS restores verification automatically. Account creation reads
the current switch under a shared transaction lock; a stale form or forged
client flag cannot retain the bypass. Provider outages and missing settings do
not bypass verification. Password reset remains protected while SMS is paused.

The public pay-site form and router captive form share atomic account creation,
phone-bound single-use OTP tokens, rate limits and duplicate protection. Accounts
start unpaid with zero wallet balance and the existing two-device policy. Welcome
SMS delivery failures cannot turn a committed registration into a reported failure.

Flight now has a prominent portal navigation entry, an overview card, and a
persistent, nonmodal captive status card. Discovery still respects manager
readiness and ghost mode. Stakes, payouts and play authorization are unchanged.

Game management, withdrawals, SMS gateways and customer registration now share
a responsive admin shell, consistent navigation and polished form/table styling.
Existing action handlers, permissions, CSRF tokens and financial controls remain.
